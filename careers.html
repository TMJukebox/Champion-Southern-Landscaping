We're looking for an experienced crew leader who can run a maintenance route, keep the crew moving, handle the customer on site, and leave every property looking sharp.

What you'll do

  • Lead a maintenance crew on residential and commercial routes
  • Mow, edge, trim, and detail properties to a high standard
  • Prune shrubs and small trees; manage beds, mulch, and weed control
  • Run and maintain mowers, trimmers, blowers, and the truck and trailer
  • Plan the day's stops and keep the crew on schedule
  • Communicate with customers on site and flag extra work to the office
  • Leave every job site clean and safe

What we're looking for

  • 2+ years of landscape maintenance experience, some in a lead role
  • Comfortable running commercial mowing equipment
  • Working knowledge of South Texas turf, plants, and irrigation basics
  • Dependable, on time, and professional with customers
  • Valid driver's license and a clean driving record (trailer experience a plus)
  • Able to work outdoors in Texas heat and lift 50+ lbs
  • Bilingual (English/Spanish) a plus

What we offer

  • Competitive pay based on experience
  • Steady, year-round work
  • Room to grow into an account or operations role
  • Family-oriented team environment
  • A mix of residential and commercial properties
  • Truck and equipment provided
